Hello!

I'm Jiahui, an MSc student in Financial Statistics at the London School of Economics, graduating in March 2027. Before LSE, I read Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Edinburgh, with an exchange year at City University of Hong Kong.

I work on intelligent systems that interact with people. My research combines statistical machine learning, language models, and human–AI interaction to study how AI can support learning and decision-making. I am particularly interested in when an AI system should intervene, when it should defer, and how its behaviour changes what people learn, choose, and trust.

Outside of research I build things. I'm the founder of YNotes, an AI-native iPad notebook now on the App Store, and I maintain Paper Signal, an open-source Agent Skills pack for editorial image production. I also built YanChat, a web client that rebuilds the GPT experience around better typography and reasoning that doesn't time out.

On the side, I publish literary fiction in Chinese under the pen name Yanchi.

Research

Calibrate Once, Choose the Beam: A Predictive Regime Test for Same-LM Search Guidance and Pruning Accepted
Jiahui Qu, Yifang Qin, Boyang Zheng, Ziyi Zhou
ICML 2026 Workshop on Language Models for Planning (LM4Plan)
A predictive regime test based on a single scalar (evaluator precision p) that decides, before deployment, whether beam search guided by the same language model outperforms independent sampling on planning tasks. Validated on maze-planning with Qwen2.5-7B / 3B.
OpenReview
Calibrate Once, Choose the Beam: A Pre-Deployment Compute-Allocation Rule for Same-LM Multimodal Search Accepted
Jiahui Qu, Yifang Qin, Boyang Zheng, Ziyi Zhou
ICML 2026 Workshop on Scaling Compute Efficiency in LLMs (SCALE)
A pre-deployment rule for allocating inference-time compute between beam search and independent sampling in same-LM multimodal search. Extends the planning-only regime test to a vision–language setting and calibrates once per model rather than per task.
OpenReview
Screen, Confirm, Then Search: Risk-Controlled Pre-Deployment Qualification for LM-Guided Search Under review
Jiahui Qu, Boyang Zheng, Yifang Qin, Yudi Cao, Jiayu Fang
Manuscript under review at TMLR
Full-paper expansion of the ICML workshop paper. Formalises a three-stage qualification pipeline (screen, confirm, search) with distribution-free risk-controlled guarantees before deploying language-model guided search in production settings.
When Better Scores Select Worse Boards: Replacement-Level Audits for Teamfight Tactics Search Under review
Jiahui Qu, Shizhao Xiong, Jiayu Fang, Tiyao Liu, Yifang Qin, Rong Fu, Haoran Liu, Xuanbo Su, Boyang Zheng
Introduces replacement-level audits for evaluating LM-guided search algorithms. Shows that higher-scoring candidates can be strictly worse than a baseline pick once evaluator drift is accounted for, with a case study on Teamfight Tactics augment selection.
When Evidence Drifts: Evidence-State Routing and Guarded Revision Release for Long-Form Factual Generation Under review
Jiahui Qu, Shizhao Xiong, Yifang Qin, Xue Mi, Jiayuan Di, Jiayu Fang, Yilan Wei, Xuanbo Su, Boyang Zheng, Ruby Cao
A framework for long-form factual generation that tracks evidence states across revisions and gates when updates are safe to release, reducing hallucinations under drifting evidence.
Lasso-to-Learn: Source-Anchored AI Practice Loops for Exam-Based Learning In preparation
Jiahui Qu
Targeting CHI 2027
Reimagines AI help in study apps as source-anchored, editable learning traces. Students lasso a stuck point in a PDF, pick a help intensity, save AI responses back into the source document, and turn solved problems into review cards and new handwritten practice.
