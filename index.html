Hello!

I'm Jiahui, an MSc student in Financial Statistics at the London School of Economics, graduating in July 2026. Before LSE, I read Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Edinburgh, where I graduated with First Class Honours.

I work at the intersection of statistics, language models, and the things people actually use. My current research focuses on inference-time compute. I'm interested in when spending more computation at test time pays off, and when it doesn't.

Outside of research I build products. I'm the founder of YNotes, an AI-native iPad notebook now on the App Store, and I'm currently developing GhostWind, a real-time conversation assistant for macOS. I also serve as Chief Strategy Officer at an AI-focused MCN company in Wuhan.

On the side, I publish literary fiction in Chinese under the pen name Yanchi (言迟).
